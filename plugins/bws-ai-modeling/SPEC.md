# BWS AI Modeling target contract

The source design is maintained in `bwc-ai-plugin-spec.md`. Its intended architecture is a parametric, revisioned recipe with numeric measurement and QA before rendering. The target includes scene/object revision APIs, atomic batches, distance/clearance/thickness/symmetry measurements, manifold and normals QA, bounded render passes, history, and isolated variants.

Version 1 of this plugin exposes the safe BWS operations that currently exist. Unimplemented target tools are not advertised to AI clients.
