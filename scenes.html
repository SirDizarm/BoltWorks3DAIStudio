01 / THE HEARTH
Interactive fireplace

Fireplace playground

A brick-and-stone hearth with three log arrangements, adjustable flames and lighting, and synthesized crackling sound. Fullscreen leaves just the fireplace.

Download Fireplace plugin

In BWS 50, use Toolbars > Scenes > Add scene plugin. Install and enable it to open the fireplace. It is not included in your active editor by default.
