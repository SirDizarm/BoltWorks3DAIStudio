# Natural stones and irregular soot - v49.64.91

Natural Stone nodes now build closed convex hulls from seeded irregular support points. Boulder, fieldstone, slate and crag retain distinct proportions, but crags have a broader, less tower-like silhouette. Irregularity changes coherent shape lobes and surface variation. Bases sit at zero. Existing detailed-rock recipes automatically regenerate when their cached Scene Studio assets predate this revision.

Scene grime now uses domain-warped isotropic noise instead of vertically stretched streaks. Charcoal grain, ember seams and burn-mask variation use non-periodic noise rather than axis-aligned sine bands. No browser tests performed.
